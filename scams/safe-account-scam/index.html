Your “bank” called and told you to move your money?

A caller says they're from your bank or the police, that your account is at risk, and that you must move your money to a “safe account”. There is no such thing. Describe what happened below to check it.

What this scam usually looks like

Phone call from “your bank”Call from my bank: they said there is fraud on my account and I need to move my savings to a new safe account today.

Red flags

  • You're told to move money to keep it safe
  • They say it's urgent and to keep it secret, even from bank staff
  • The number on your phone looks like your bank's (numbers can be faked)
  • They ask for a code that's just been texted to you

What to do

  1. Hang up. Your bank will never ask you to move money to a safe account.
  2. Call 159 to reach your real bank safely, ideally from a different phone.
  3. Never share codes texted to you, or install apps a caller asks for.
  4. If you've lost money, call your bank straight away, then report it to Report Fraud (formerly Action Fraud) at reportfraud.police.uk or on 0300 123 2040.

Questions

What is 159?

159 is a free number that connects you to your own bank's fraud team, so you know you're talking to the real bank.

Will I get my money back?

Since October 2024, UK banks must reimburse most victims of this kind of push-payment fraud. Call your bank straight away.
