Red flags in business payment scams

  • A supplier says their bank details have changed, by email only
  • The sender's address is slightly different from usual, such as a missing letter or .co instead of .co.uk
  • Urgency or secrecy: “pay today”, “keep this between us”
  • “The boss” asks for a quick transfer or gift cards while they're unreachable
  • An invoice you weren't expecting, or a new payee name

Simple rules that stop most of them

  1. Confirm any change of bank details by phone, on a number you already hold.
  2. Use Confirmation of Payee, and pause if the name doesn't match.
  3. Require a second person to approve new or changed payees.
  4. If money has gone, call your bank immediately, then report it to Report Fraud at reportfraud.police.uk or on 0300 123 2040.
