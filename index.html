Scams going round the UK right now

If you've had one of these, you're not alone. Tap “Check one like this” to see how KinAlert reads it, or read the full guides.

  • “Add my number on WhatsApp” callsA bot calls and asks you to add a number. It leads to fake job offers and money requests.
  • “Hi Mum, new number”Someone pretends to be your child on a new number and asks for money today.
  • The “safe account” callA caller says they're your bank and tells you to move your money to keep it safe. Banks never do this.
  • Parcel fee textsA text says a parcel is held until you pay a small fee through a link.
  • Tax refund textsA message says HMRC owes you money and you must claim it today.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Checking a message is free. We're building a paid family plan that alerts you when your parent gets a scam.

What happens to what I send?

Your message or screenshot is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to be checked, then the answer comes back to you. KinAlert doesn't save it, and we never contact you or the sender. Don't send passwords, PINs or full card numbers.

Can it be wrong?

Yes, it can. It's built to be careful: if it isn't sure, it says so rather than calling something safe. If money is involved, hang up and call your bank on 159.

What should I do if it's a scam?

Don't reply, click links or send money. Forward scam texts to 7726 for free. If you've lost money, call your bank straight away and report it to Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040.

Who's behind KinAlert?

KinAlert is being built in the UK by a 21-year-old founder who gets these scam calls and texts constantly, and started worrying how easily his own mum could fall for one.
